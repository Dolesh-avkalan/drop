# Relay check t009 — History of paper

Three facts about the history of paper:

- **Paper was invented in China.** Tradition credits the Han court official Cai Lun, who around 105 CE standardised a method of making paper from mulberry bark, rags, hemp and fishing nets — an improvement on earlier rough paper-like sheets.
- **Papermaking spread to the Islamic world.** After the Battle of Talas in 751 CE, the technique is said to have reached Samarkand and then Baghdad, from where it travelled across the Middle East and North Africa.
- **Paper reached Europe through Muslim Spain.** A paper mill is recorded at Xàtiva in the 12th century, and papermaking gradually replaced parchment and papyrus across medieval Europe.
